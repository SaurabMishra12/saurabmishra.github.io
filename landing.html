After all that compute, what comes out? Six manuscripts under review or in preparation, a club of two hundred people I started and then handed on, a hackathon for a thousand, and the social habit of explaining architectures to audiences who did not ask.

Curiosity Is All You Need

The full architecture, drawn the way papers draw them. I submitted myself to this pipeline in 2024 and it has not converged yet.

The diagram is the page. Each block is one of the five layers above, in the order you just read them, wrapped in the residual stream that carries everything forward.

What I actually study is what happens inside these blocks — whether the representations they build have geometric structure you can describe rather than just visualise, whether sudden jumps in capability behave like phase transitions, and whether any of it can be derived rather than ablated.

Concretely, that means small open models, pretrained checkpoints and activation analysis, because that is what fits on the hardware I have. The constraint turns out to pick better questions than I would have picked without it.
